Mode Switch helps you automate your home based on clear modes such as Home, Away, Sleep and Vacation. You choose which devices belong to a mode, and the app switches them for you so your home reacts consistently without building every Flow manually.

The app also gives you practical widgets for mode control, monitoring and appliance tracking. For selected appliances you can see when they are running, how long they have been active and how much energy they have used, and use that information in Flows.

Dashboard API (3.0.1)
---------------------
Mode Switch exposes a read-only dashboard status endpoint for optional use by Home Overview. It includes modes, monitored appliances, activities and the next known schedule.
