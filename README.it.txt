Modalita Switch helps you automate your Casa based on clear Modalita such as Casa, Fuori casa, Sonno and Vacanza. You choose which Dispositivi belong to a Modalita, and the app switches them for you so your Casa reacts consistently without building every Flow manually.

The app also gives you practical widgets for Modalita control, Monitoraggio and appliance tracking. For selected appliances you can see when they are In esecuzione, how long they have been active and how much energy they have used, and use that information in Flows.

Dashboard API (3.0.1)
---------------------
Modalita Switch exposes a read-only dashboard status endpoint for optional use by Casa Overview. It includes Modalita, monitored appliances, Attivita and the next known Pianificazione.
