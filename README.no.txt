Modus Switch helps you automate your Hjemme based on clear Moduser such as Hjemme, Borte, Sove and Ferie. You choose which Enheter belong to a Modus, and the app switches them for you so your Hjemme reacts consistently without building every Flow manually.

The app also gives you practical widgets for Modus control, Overvaking and appliance tracking. For selected appliances you can see when they are Kjorer, how long they have been active and how much energy they have used, and use that information in Flows.

Dashboard API (3.0.1)
---------------------
Modus Switch exposes a read-only dashboard status endpoint for optional use by Hjemme Overview. It includes Moduser, monitored appliances, Aktiviteter and the next known Planlegging.
