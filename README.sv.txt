Lage Switch helps you automate your Hemma based on clear Lagen such as Hemma, Borta, Sova and Semester. You choose which Enheter belong to a Lage, and the app switches them for you so your Hemma reacts consistently without building every Flow manually.

The app also gives you practical widgets for Lage control, Overvakning and appliance tracking. For selected appliances you can see when they are Kors, how long they have been active and how much energy they have used, and use that information in Flows.

Dashboard API (3.0.1)
---------------------
Lage Switch exposes a read-only dashboard status endpoint for optional use by Hemma Overview. It includes Lagen, monitored appliances, Aktiviteter and the next known Schema.
